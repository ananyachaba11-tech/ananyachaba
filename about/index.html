Some data-stuff I have learnt along the way:

I’ve learned firsthand that data reflects how it is collected and produced. I treat a dataset as a source, not the source of truth.

Working with messy datasets has taught me patience and the importance of documenting your decisions.

Working with statistics has taught me how to choose the analytical approach that best fits the question.

Using code to find and build datasets has shown me that data can hide in unexpected places.

My research background has taught me to hold my work to high standards of methodological rigour, integrity, and transparency.

I believe good data work is as much about knowing what you cannot claim as it is about finding what you can.
