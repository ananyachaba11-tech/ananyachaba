An investigation into Indian youth football

This project is currently being developed for publication. I’m sharing the reporting question, process and skills behind the investigation here, while keeping the central findings, detailed methodology and underlying dataset private.

What is this project?

India’s football federation has laid out ambitious plans for the development of youth football in its Vision 2047 roadmap. I wanted to understand what progress towards those ambitions looks like.

What was I trying to find out?

How many league matches did each youth male player play during the 2025–26 Indian football season?

What kind of data did I work with?

There was no single downloadable dataset that could answer this question. I used coding techniques to turn records spread across systems into a structured dataset.

What data techniques did I use?

  • Undocumented APIs
  • Python and Pandas
  • LLM-assisted classification
  • Datawrapper, Illustrator, and web scrollytelling
  • …and lots of manual work!
